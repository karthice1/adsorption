SHA-256 checksums are in `SHA256SUMS` (regenerated by `scripts/run_v2.py --stage chem`;
verify with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`). The files are used unmodified. They are distributed
with USGS PHREEQC, a U.S. Government work in the public domain.

The phreeqpython-bundled databases (phreeqc.dat, wateq4f_PWN.dat, vitens.dat, pitzer.dat)
contain no chromium species and are rejected by `cassia.chemistry.engine`.
